Inside the science Retrieval · paired experiments · calibration

Memory, with a reason to retrieve

Score memories by recency, importance and semantic relevance. Reflection compresses experience into beliefs; planning converts beliefs into actions under resource constraints.

R(m) = wᵣ recency + wᵢ importance + wₛ relevance

Measure the intervention

For each seed, replay the same population under control and treatment. The paired difference isolates the modeled rule change. Repeat across seeds and agent assumptions.

Δₛ = metric(treatment, s) − metric(control, s)

Validate against the world

Use time-separated and creator-disjoint holdouts. Compare against simple baselines. Score only externally observed outcomes; abstain when data or cohort coverage is insufficient.

Brier = mean[(pᵢ − yᵢ)²], yᵢ ∈ {0,1}

Agent simulations test conditional hypotheses. A simulated intervention is not a measured real-world causal effect. Market prices remain sensitive to liquidity, risk and selection.

Inside the market Collateral · royalties · instrument boundaries

Binary accounting

Each complete YES/NO pair is backed by $1. Trades transfer claims; fees are separate cash entries. A matched fill and its position, balance and fee updates must commit atomically.

P&L = exit value − entry cost − trading fees

Aligned creator economics

A consenting profile receives a pre-agreed fee share; a PK market can allocate that share between both profiles. Block creator and affiliate trading in their own outcomes. Verify identity and detect wash activity.

royalty = eligible collected fees × agreed share

Perpetuals are a separate system

Continuous exposure requires a robust index, margin, funding, liquidation and loss-absorption rules. Funding links the traded price to the index and transfers between longs and shorts; it is not automatically platform revenue.

P&L = signed quantity × Δcontract price − net funding − fees

Current executable Backer flows use fictional profiles and simulated credits. Real-money execution, creator royalty contracts and perpetual markets are future product layers.

01 / DATA ADVANTAGE

History is hard to recreate.

Public profiles are easy to copy. A prospective history of elicited forecasts, observed commitments and independently verified outcomes takes repeated participation and time.

02 / MODEL ADVANTAGE

Errors become specific.

Outcome labels reveal where a model fails: a cohort, a time horizon, a liquidity regime. Targeted corrections are more useful than collecting more unlabeled activity.

03 / PRODUCT ADVANTAGE

Better tests can improve markets.

Stress-tested rules can reduce poor fills and ambiguous settlement. Better market quality can earn repeat participation and more useful observations.
